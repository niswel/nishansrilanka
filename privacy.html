Information you send

If you use the contact form, you may provide your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, travel dates, number of travellers and trip details. This information is used to respond to your enquiry and to discuss or arrange the requested travel service.

Contact form

The contact form is processed by Formspree so that enquiries can be delivered to Nishan. Formspree may process the information you submit in order to provide this service. Formspree operates under its own privacy policy and terms.

Security and Cloudflare Turnstile

The contact form uses Cloudflare Turnstile for spam and automated-abuse protection. Turnstile may process technical and browser information needed to determine whether a form submission is legitimate. This security technology is used to protect the form and is not used by Nishan as an optional analytics or advertising service.

Cookies and similar technologies

This website uses a small consent record in your browser's local storage to remember whether you accepted, rejected or customised optional technologies. This record is necessary for the consent system itself and does not contain the information you submit through the contact form.

Optional Google technologies are controlled by your cookie preferences:

  • Analytics: Google Analytics 4 may be used to understand how visitors use the website and to improve its content and performance. Analytics storage is enabled only when you allow the Analytics category.
  • Advertising: Google advertising measurement and related advertising features may be used when you allow the Advertising category. Advertising storage, advertising-related user data and advertising personalisation are controlled through the consent settings.

The website uses Google Consent Mode so that the consent choices made through the banner are communicated to Google. Before an optional category is allowed, the corresponding Google consent signals are set to denied. If you later change your preferences, the consent settings are updated accordingly.

Managing or withdrawing consent

You can change your optional cookie choices at any time by selecting Cookie Settings in the website footer. You can also clear your browser's site data, after which the consent banner will appear again.

WhatsApp, Google and other external services

This website contains links to WhatsApp, Google services and other external websites, including Nishan's Google Business Profile and SunSpire Holidays. Those services operate under their own privacy policies, terms and cookie practices. Clicking an external link takes you to the relevant third-party service.

Language preference

The language selector may use browser local storage to remember your selected website language. This is used only to maintain the website's language preference and is not used for advertising or analytics.
